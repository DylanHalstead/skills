# Sources and adaptation boundaries

This skill synthesizes visual-direction concepts in new wording for collaborative
design work. These links record the material used to maintain the guidance;
they are optional background, not runtime dependencies. No upstream engine,
hook, command workflow, or asset library is included.

## Impeccable

[Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/tree/c63b8dd7dd5a5f3c32eaabe1af76d0694f863ed2),
by Paul Bakaus (Apache-2.0), is the primary conceptual source. Relevant material:

- [Main skill](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c63b8dd7dd5a5f3c32eaabe1af76d0694f863ed2/.agents/skills/impeccable/SKILL.md): brief authority, preservation versus replacement, and surface purposes.
- [New work](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c63b8dd7dd5a5f3c32eaabe1af76d0694f863ed2/.agents/skills/impeccable/reference/new-work.md): direction grounded in the subject and audience rather than a category template.

The adaptation replaces tool-driven concept selection, mandatory confirmations,
project setup, and aesthetic bans with scoped proposals and explicit trade-offs.
It does not reproduce those playbooks. The [source license](../licenses/impeccable-Apache-2.0.txt)
is retained with its copyright notice.

## Taste

[Taste v2](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/717446e07a78d4e6d7918b1fde1a263378eebac9/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md),
by Leonxlnx (MIT), informs diagnostics for repetitive composition, generic imagery,
and unmotivated motion. These are questions about fit, not bans on particular
patterns. Numeric dials, stack defaults, required imagery, and preflight quotas
are not adopted. The [source license](../licenses/taste-MIT.txt) is retained.

## Ownership

UI usability and detailed accessibility remain in `user-interface-design`.
Implementation mechanics remain in `frontend-development`. New examples here
illustrate adaptable reasoning, not prescribed layouts, fonts, or dependencies.
